I am a technology executive with over two decades of experience shaping large-scale digital transformation across financial services. As VP of Information Technology at Barclays, I architect and deliver complex platforms spanning wholesale lending, securitized products, and structured finance.

My work sits at the convergence of cloud architecture, data engineering, and enterprise risk analytics — translating regulatory imperatives and commercial ambition into resilient, scalable infrastructure.

I believe the most consequential technology decisions are not purely technical — they are organizational, cultural, and strategic. My approach is to build platforms that outlast the initiatives that inspired them.
