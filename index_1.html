I am a senior technology leader with 20 years of experience delivering large-scale digital transformation across global financial institutions. At Barclays Capital, I architect and deliver mission-critical capital markets platforms spanning loan trading, securitization, and mortgage analytics.

My work sits at the convergence of cloud-native engineering, event-driven architecture, and AI-ready data platforms — translating complex business and regulatory requirements into resilient, scalable infrastructure. I have led global engineering organizations, influenced executive stakeholders and regulators, and driven enterprise modernization at scale.

A recognized thought leader and keynote speaker, I bring together deep technical expertise and executive perspective to build platforms — and engineering cultures — that outlast the initiatives that created them.
